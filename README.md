# 🏃‍♂️ The First Mile Club — Hyderabad

**From the developer:** Crafted with pure HTML & CSS, featuring Fraunces and Space Grotesk typography, a soft lavender-coral palette, subtle animations, and a responsive layout that feels polished on every screen.

**Pace doesn't matter, vibes do.**

A simple, welcoming website for **The First Mile Club** — a run & social club based in Hyderabad. We meet, we run (or walk), and we stick around afterward because the best conversations usually start after the first mile.

### 🌐 Live Site
**[firstmileclub.onrender.com](https://firstmileclub.onrender.com)**

### Why this exists
The first mile is often the hardest part — of a run, of meeting new people, of showing up. This site introduces the club, shares the story behind it, and points you toward the community (mostly on Instagram) where the real energy lives.

No fitness tests. No pressure. Just good people and good vibes.

### Come say hi
Curious? Head over to the site or follow along on Instagram [@first_mileclub](https://www.instagram.com/first_mileclub/).  
Show up whenever you're ready — all paces are welcome. ✨
